Privacy Policy

Dreamara · Krigoda · Last updated: June 2025

Short version: Dreamara does not collect, store, or share your personal data. The app works fully offline. We have no access to your device or usage.

1. Who We Are

Dreamara is developed and operated by Krigoda. For privacy-related questions, contact us at dreamara@krigoda.com.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, or store:

— Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
— Your location or device identifiers
— Your usage patterns, search queries, or dream history
— Any analytics or crash reporting data

All app data (favourites, recently viewed terms, settings) is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted anywhere.

3. In-App Purchases — RevenueCat

Dreamara uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat may collect:

— A hashed device identifier to verify purchase status
— Purchase transaction data (product ID, timestamp, platform)
— App version and platform information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details — those remain with Apple or Google. For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Purchases themselves are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play and are subject to their privacy policies.

4. No Third-Party Analytics

Dreamara does not use any analytics services (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar). We have no visibility into how you use the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Dreamara does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The app contains no features that require or encourage personal data submission.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As we do not collect personal data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you have made an in-app purchase and wish to exercise data rights related to RevenueCat's processing, please contact dreamara@krigoda.com and we will assist you.

7. Data Retention

All data stored by the app (favourites, settings, history) resides on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. We retain no copies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality changes in ways that affect data processing. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
