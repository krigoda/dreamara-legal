Need help? Write to us at dreamara@krigoda.com — we usually reply within 2 business days. You can write in English, Croatian, German or Slovenian.

Restore my Dreamara Pro purchase

If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new phone, open Settings → About → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dreamara Pro.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Dreamara Pro.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them:
Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
Google Play: Google Play refund help

Change the language

In Settings you can choose the app language and, separately, the dictionary language (English, Croatian, German or Slovenian).

Does the app need internet?

No. The whole dictionary is stored on your device and works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to make or restore a purchase.
